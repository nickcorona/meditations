# Meditations

## replaying-criticism
for: replaying what someone said about me
idea: Their opinion lives in their head, not mine. Keep what's useful, drop the rest.
why: Marcus 12.4: we care more about others' opinions of us than our own.
stage: read

## dreading-the-day
for: lying in bed not wanting to start
idea: Get up and do the work of a person. Feet on the floor is the whole first step.
why: Marcus 5.1. The days I start before I feel ready go better than the ones I wait out.
stage: read

## circling-a-task
for: circling a task I keep putting off
idea: Do two minutes of it now. Open the thing and write one line.
stage: read

## heat-at-someone
for: heat rising at how someone's acting
idea: They're doing what seems right to them. I've been that wrong too.
why: Marcus 2.1: expect the meddling and rude, and remember they're my kin.
stage: read

## spinning-on-outcomes
for: spinning on something I can't control
idea: Split it into what's mine and what isn't. Do mine, let the rest land.
stage: read

## measuring-against-others
for: measuring my life against someone else's
idea: Look at my own lane. Am I better than I was last month?
why: Marcus 4.18: how much time he gains who doesn't look at what his neighbour says or does.
stage: read

## lost-an-hour
for: realizing I just lost an hour scrolling
idea: That hour's gone. Don't spend the next one on guilt. Pick the next thing.
why: Marcus 2.14: the present is all anyone has to lose.
stage: read

## making-it-huge
for: treating something small like it's huge
idea: In a year nobody, me included, will remember this.
why: Marcus 4.32: think of all the busy lives before mine, and where they are now.
stage: read

## picturing-the-worst
for: running every way something could go wrong
idea: Stick to what's actually in front of me. Which part of right now can't I handle?
why: Marcus 8.36: don't picture everything bad that could happen; ask why this moment is unbearable.
stage: read

## plan-fell-through
for: stuck because the plan fell through
idea: The obstacle is the next thing to work on. What stands in the way becomes the way.
why: Marcus 5.20.
stage: read

## wanting-approval
for: wanting someone's approval
idea: Look at who they actually are. Would I trust their judgment on anything else?
why: Marcus 7.62: look at the people whose approval you want, and what their minds are like.
stage: read

## no-credit
for: annoyed nobody noticed or said thanks
idea: Doing it was the point. Eyes don't ask to be paid for seeing.
why: Marcus 9.42 and 5.6: the vine makes grapes and doesn't ask for anything back.
stage: read

## bracing-for-people
for: bracing for a day of difficult people
idea: Some of them will be annoying today. Still on the same team.
why: Marcus 2.1: the meddling and ungrateful are built to work with me, like two rows of teeth.
stage: read

## too-proud-to-ask
for: stuck on something and not wanting to ask
idea: Send the message asking for help. Wounded soldiers get pulled up the wall.
why: Marcus 7.7: don't be ashamed to need help.
stage: read

## too-big-to-start
for: overwhelmed by how big the whole thing is
idea: Write down the next single step and do only that one.
why: Marcus 6.26: spell the name one letter at a time. 8.32: assemble the life action by action.
stage: read

## defensive-when-wrong
for: getting defensive when someone says I'm wrong
idea: If they're right, I get to be less wrong. The truth never hurt anyone.
why: Marcus 6.21.
stage: read

## hard-means-impossible
for: telling myself I can't do it because it's hard
idea: Hard isn't impossible. If a person can do it, I can practice it.
why: Marcus 6.19, and 12.6: the left hand holds the reins better than the right, from practice.
stage: read

## replaying-my-mistake
for: replaying a mistake I made
idea: Get back up and do the next right thing. I don't need a clean record, just the next rep.
why: Marcus 5.9: don't be despondent when your days aren't full of good actions; get back up.
stage: read

## wanting-what-i-dont-have
for: wanting the thing I don't have
idea: Picture losing what I already have. Value that, without clinging to it.
why: Marcus 7.27.
stage: read

## planning-instead-of-doing
for: reading and planning instead of doing
idea: Stop researching what good looks like. Do the first piece now.
why: Marcus 10.16: stop talking about what the good man is like, and just be one.
stage: read

# Meditations

Ranked by usefulness, most useful first. Cue Home introduces them in this order, then tests them with spaced repetition.

## circling-a-task
for: circling a task I keep putting off
idea: Two minutes on it, right now.
stage: read

## too-big-to-start
for: overwhelmed by how big the whole thing is
idea: Just the next step.
why: One letter at a time. (6.26)
stage: read

stage: read

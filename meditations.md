# Meditations

Ranked by usefulness, most useful first. Cue Home introduces them in this order, then tests them with spaced repetition.

## circling-a-task
for: circling a task I keep putting off
idea: I could be good today. Instead I keep choosing tomorrow. Two minutes, now.
why: (8.22a)
stage: read

## too-big-to-start
for: overwhelmed by how big the whole thing is
idea: Spell the name one letter at a time.
why: Big things are just small steps in order. (6.26)
stage: read

## planning-instead-of-doing
